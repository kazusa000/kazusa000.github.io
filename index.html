01 / RESEARCH研究

Current research当前研究

CURRENT · DOCTORAL RESEARCH当前 · 博士研究

Transformations for
feedback control面向反馈控制
的系统变换

How can we transform a dynamical system into a target whose behavior we understand? I work on constructive approaches to this question, studying the transformation together with the feedback that makes it possible.怎样把动力系统变成行为更易分析的目标系统？我研究这一问题的构造方法，同时求解系统变换与实现它的反馈。

My present focus is on finite-dimensional linear systems with single or multiple inputs. I am preparing the theoretical work as a manuscript and beginning numerical comparisons, including invertible neural networks for computing transformations.目前聚焦有限维线性系统的单输入与多输入情形。我正在将理论工作整理成论文，并开始比较数值求解方法，包括使用可逆神经网络计算变换。
